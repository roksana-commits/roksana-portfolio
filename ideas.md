## Selected Direction: Signal & Structure

**Design Movement:** Contemporary Swiss editorial design blended with information-design principles and restrained digital brutalism.

**Core Principles:**
1. Evidence before ornament: performance metrics and skills are presented as proof, not decoration.
2. Editorial asymmetry: a strong left rail and offset content blocks create a portfolio that reads like a considered case file rather than a generic centered landing page.
3. Calm precision: a warm ivory canvas, ink-black typography, and a single ownable blue accent create confidence without visual noise.
4. Human signal: Roksana’s portrait and direct first-person copy keep the analytical work approachable.

**Color Philosophy:** The foundation is warm ivory rather than stark white, reflecting a research notebook and reducing visual fatigue. Deep ink provides authority and legibility. The signature color is **Roksana Blue** (#2D5BFF), a clear, active blue that communicates digital fluency and forward motion. Terracotta is used sparingly as a human counterpoint in data labels and small highlights.

**Layout Paradigm:** A responsive editorial rail: desktop layouts use a narrow, persistent left-side identity rail and a wide reading canvas with offset sections. Metrics sit in a horizontal proof band; skill groups are arranged as ranked evidence rather than uniform cards. On mobile, the rail becomes a compact top bar and the reading order stays linear.

**Signature Elements:**
- A blue vertical rule and small monospaced section labels that behave like editorial margin notes.
- Large typographic numerals for proof metrics, paired with thin progress indicators.
- A compact “signal index” motif using blue bars and tiny data ticks beside key skills.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions should feel like revealing evidence. Anchor navigation scrolls to sections, buttons have a decisive press state, and skill rows lift only slightly on hover. There are no decorative carousels or hidden menus; every interactive element should clarify how to contact Roksana or understand her capabilities.

**Animation:** Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions for links, buttons, and skill rows. On first load, reveal the identity rail, headline, and proof band in a 50ms stagger. Animate only opacity and transform. Avoid continuous motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling entrance transitions.

**Typography System:** Use **DM Sans** for the body and interface, with **Space Grotesk** for display headlines and large metrics. Use **IBM Plex Mono** for section labels, metadata, and small data annotations. Headlines should be tight and left-aligned; body copy should stay between 16–19px with generous line-height. Metrics may use a heavier display weight but never all-caps.

**Brand Essence:** A research-minded digital operator who turns messy information into usable momentum for teams that need clean data, dependable execution, and technical range.  
**Personality:** Precise, resourceful, grounded.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are direct and specific; CTAs sound like an invitation to work, not a sales funnel. Microcopy is factual, warm, and lightly confident.  
Example headline: “Clear answers from complicated data.”  
Example CTA: “Bring me the messy part.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A compact geometric mark built from two offset blue brackets forming an abstract “R” and an open data pathway. The wordmark uses a custom-spaced uppercase “ROKSANA” lockup with a small monospaced descriptor beneath; the mark can stand alone as the favicon.

**Signature Brand Color:** Roksana Blue — `#2D5BFF`.

## Style Decisions

- Use the Signal & Structure direction consistently across the site.
- Keep the primary page left-aligned and editorial; avoid a generic centered hero.
- Use supplied portrait material as the main human visual and generated graphics only for restrained abstract data texture or brand marking.
- Preserve a warm ivory background, ink text, Roksana Blue accents, and mono metadata.
